The curve is the story's path through embedding space, fitted through windows of consecutive paragraphs (dots), from O to X. Every window has a cross-section: one corner per emotion, further out the stronger it is — or, for paragraph spread, how scattered that window's paragraphs are around the curve. The sections are joined smoothly in reading order.
Where the curve bends tighter than the tube is thick, the whole tube is scaled down by one factor and only the sharpest bends are narrowed further, so thickness stays comparable along the story. The header reports both.
